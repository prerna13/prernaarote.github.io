About me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at IMDEA Networks. My research focuses on the security and privacy of decentralized systems, with interests spanning blockchain security, decentralized finance, privacy‑preserving protocols, and secure digital infrastructure.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), where my research focused on enhancing the security and efficiency of blockchain‑based micropayments, payment channels, and IoT applications.

You can find me at prerna.arote@networks.imdea.org.
