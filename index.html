I am a postdoctoral researcher at IMDEA Networks Institute, working on secure, reliable, and privacy-preserving distributed systems.

My research focuses on the security of decentralized systems, with interests spanning blockchain security, decentralized finance, privacy-preserving protocols, and secure digital infrastructure.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), where my research focused on enhancing the security and efficiency of blockchain-based micropayments, payment channels, and IoT applications.
